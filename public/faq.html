Frequently asked questions

Answers about playing Lights Out Mafia.

What is Lights Out Mafia?
Lights Out Mafia is a social deduction party game for 4 to 12 players. The Mafia secretly removes players at night while the town discusses clues and votes during the day.
How do I start a game?
Choose a name, select Open a room, and share the five-letter room code or link with your friends. Players can join from their own devices.
Can we play without accounts?
Yes. No account, email address, or password is required.
Can we play on one phone?
Yes. Choose Play on one phone and pass the device around, or let one person act as the referee.
How many players do we need?
A multiplayer game needs at least 4 players and supports up to 12. A referee does not count as a player.
What roles are included?
Games can include Mafia, a Detective, a Doctor, and Townspeople. The role mix changes with the number of players.
Does Lights Out work on phones?
Yes. It is a progressive web app designed for modern mobile and desktop browsers and can be added to a device home screen.
