Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Lights Out Mafia is designed to work without accounts. This policy explains the limited information used to operate the game.

Information the game uses

Game information: A room code, the names players choose, role assignments, moves, and room state are processed so the game can run. Names and game state are visible to the players in that room as part of gameplay.

Technical information: The server may receive an IP address and normal request information. IP addresses are used for connection limits and abuse prevention, not for advertising or user profiling.

On your device: The app may store a chosen name, game preferences, an in-progress one-phone game, and installed-app files in browser storage. You can remove this data through your browser settings.

How information is used

Information is used to host rooms, synchronize players, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and keep the service secure. Lights Out Mafia does not sell personal information and does not use advertising trackers.

Storage and retention

Room data is stored in the application database when a database is configured. Finished game records may be retained for operational purposes; the default retention setting is 30 days. Active rooms expire according to the server configuration.

Service providers

The hosting and database providers selected by the operator may process the information needed to run the service. They are used as infrastructure providers, not for advertising.

Children and changes

Do not use the service if you are not allowed to use online services under the laws that apply to you. This policy may be updated when the service changes.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the operator of the deployment where you are playing. This general policy is not legal advice; the operator should replace this text with deployment-specific contact and jurisdiction details before launch.
